Cookie Policy

This page is a launch placeholder and should be reviewed against the final company details, hosting, analytics, enquiry handling and regulatory disclosures before the production website goes live.

Optional website analytics

When configured, Google Analytics is loaded only after you accept optional analytics. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the website or its calculators. Analytics helps us understand visits, pages used, calculator interactions, illustration actions and enquiry actions. Advertising features are disabled.

Your choices

Use “Analytics settings” in the footer to accept, reject or withdraw your choice. We store your choice locally in your browser for up to 180 days. This preference does not identify you to us. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice may need to be made again on another page.

Analytics cookies

If you accept and analytics is configured, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies including _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visits and support session statistics. They are configured to expire after up to 180 days without automatic extension. Withdrawing consent stops further analytics collection and clears these analytics cookies for this website.

Information excluded

Our custom tracking does not include enquiry form entries, names, email addresses, telephone numbers or calculator amounts and rates. We send page addresses without their query strings or fragments and restrict referrer information to the referring website’s origin.
