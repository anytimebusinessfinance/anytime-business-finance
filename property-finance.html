YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

What information is needed for a commercial mortgage?

Start with the property address, purchase price or estimated value, deposit, intended use and business or rental income. The provider will confirm its valuation, legal and financial requirements.

What is an exit strategy for bridging finance?

It is the planned way of repaying the facility, such as a property sale or longer-term refinance. The lender assesses whether the proposed exit and timescale are credible.

Can you explore refurbishment or development finance?

We can discuss project costs, available equity, planning position, experience, programme and intended sale or refinance. Availability depends on the project, borrower and lender criteria.
