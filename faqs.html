FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions business owners ask about finance.

Practical answers, without unnecessary jargon.

What types of finance can ABF help arrange?

We work across business loans, asset finance, property finance, working capital, revolving credit facilities and healthcare finance, together with related structured funding requirements.

Do I need to know which finance product I need?

No. Start with what you are trying to achieve. We can discuss the requirement, understand the business and identify finance structures and lenders that may fit.

How much can my business borrow?

It depends on factors including turnover, profitability, cash flow, existing borrowing, assets, credit profile, the purpose of the funding and the lender's criteria.

What information will lenders usually require?

Requirements vary, but lenders may ask for recent bank statements, filed accounts, management information, details of existing borrowing, identification documents and information about the purpose of the funding.

How quickly can business finance complete?

Timescales vary considerably by product, lender, complexity, information available and whether legal, valuation or other third-party work is required. Straightforward facilities may move quickly, while property and more structured transactions can take longer.

Does applying for finance affect my credit profile?

It can. Different lenders use different types of credit searches and assessment processes. Where a credit search is required, the type of search and its potential impact should be understood before proceeding.

Will security be required?

Not always. Some facilities are unsecured, while others require security over property, receivables, equipment or other business assets. Requirements depend on the lender, product and risk profile.

Will I need to provide a personal guarantee?

Possibly. Personal guarantees are common across parts of the commercial finance market, but requirements vary by lender, facility size, security and circumstances. The proposed guarantee should be considered as part of the overall facility terms.

What does ABF mean by structuring a facility?

It means looking beyond the amount required and considering the appropriate product, term, repayment profile, security, flexibility and lender appetite so the proposed facility fits the commercial objective.

How does ABF compare finance facilities?

We consider the wider structure, including pricing, fees, term, security, repayment profile, flexibility and relevant conditions rather than looking only at a headline interest rate.

Does ABF lend the money directly?

No. Anytime Business Finance Ltd is a commercial finance intermediary, not a lender. We introduce businesses to third-party finance providers and help manage the funding process.

How is ABF paid?

ABF may receive commission from a lender or finance provider following a successful introduction. Any fees or other remuneration relevant to a particular transaction can depend on the lender, product and circumstances.

Is funding guaranteed?

No. Finance is subject to status, eligibility, lender assessment and approval. Terms, rates and availability vary between finance providers and applicants.

Can ABF help refinance existing borrowing?

Yes. Refinancing can involve replacing, consolidating or restructuring existing facilities. The objective might be to change the repayment profile, release additional capital, simplify borrowing or move to a structure that better fits the business.

Can ABF help with tax or VAT payments?

Potentially. Business loans and other working-capital facilities may be available to help spread significant VAT, corporation tax or other business liabilities, subject to lender criteria.

Can ABF help fund an acquisition or management buyout?

Potentially. Acquisition, management buyout and share-purchase funding can involve one or several finance products depending on the target business, cash flow, assets, buyer contribution and transaction structure.

What is asset refinance?

Asset refinance raises funding against assets the business already owns. It can release capital tied up in vehicles, plant, machinery or other qualifying equipment while the business continues using those assets, subject to the facility terms.

Can I finance used equipment or vehicles?

Often, yes. Eligibility depends on the asset, its age and condition, the supplier, proposed term and lender criteria.

What is invoice finance?

Invoice finance releases funding against eligible unpaid customer invoices. It can help reduce the cash-flow gap between supplying goods or services and receiving customer payment.

What is the difference between factoring and invoice discounting?

Both can provide funding against receivables. Factoring can include credit control and collections services, while invoice discounting generally leaves collection of customer payments with the business. Exact structures vary by provider.

What is a revolving credit facility?

An RCF provides an agreed credit limit that may be drawn, repaid and potentially redrawn during the term, subject to the lender's conditions. It can be useful where working-capital requirements fluctuate.

Can ABF help with commercial property purchases?

Yes. Depending on the transaction, potential structures can include commercial mortgages, bridging finance, development finance, refurbishment finance and other property-backed facilities.

What is bridging finance used for?

Bridging finance is short-term secured funding commonly used for time-sensitive property purchases, auctions, refurbishment or situations where longer-term finance is not yet in place. A credible repayment or exit strategy is important.

Can ABF help before I bid at a property auction?

Yes. Discussing the proposed purchase and funding position before bidding can help establish what information, deposit, security and exit strategy a lender is likely to require. Finance is still subject to lender approval.

Does ABF work with healthcare businesses?

Yes. We can explore finance for healthcare businesses and practices, including acquisitions, premises, equipment, fit-outs, working capital and refinancing, subject to lender appetite and eligibility.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

We start by understanding the business, the funding requirement and what you are trying to achieve. From there, the next steps depend on the type of finance and the information needed to assess suitable routes.

Do I have to proceed after discussing funding with ABF?

No. An initial discussion does not commit you to taking a facility. Any finance you choose to pursue will be subject to the relevant lender's process, documentation and approval.

Why use a commercial finance broker?

A broker can help a business understand possible funding structures, identify lenders whose appetite may fit the requirement, present the transaction and coordinate the process. The appropriate route depends on the circumstances of each business.
