FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions business owners ask about finance.

Practical answers, without unnecessary jargon.

What types of finance can ABF help arrange?

We work across business loans, asset finance, property finance, working capital, revolving credit facilities and healthcare finance, together with related structured funding requirements.

How much can my business borrow?

It depends on turnover, profitability, cash flow, existing borrowing, assets, credit profile, purpose and the lender's criteria.

Do I need to know which finance product I need?

No. Start with the objective. We can discuss the requirement and identify structures that may fit.

How quickly can funding complete?

Timescales vary considerably by product, lender, complexity, information available and any legal or valuation work required.

Will security or a personal guarantee be required?

That depends on the lender, product, facility size and risk profile. We explain proposed security and guarantees as part of comparing a facility.

How does ABF compare facilities?

We consider the whole structure — pricing, fees, term, security, repayment profile, flexibility and how the facility fits the business.
