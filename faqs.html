Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to common commercial finance questions.

If your question is specific to your business, speak to us directly — context matters.

Do I need to know which finance product I need?

No. Tell us what you are trying to achieve and we can discuss the structures that may fit.

How much can my business borrow?

There is no universal calculation. Lenders may consider turnover, profitability, cash flow, EBITDA, existing debt, assets, security, trading history and purpose.

How quickly can finance be arranged?

Timescales vary by product and complexity. Secured and larger transactions may require valuation, legal work or due diligence.

Can I get finance if my bank has declined me?

Potentially. Lenders have different credit policies and appetites, but it is important to understand the reason for the original decline.

What information will you need?

Commonly recent bank statements, accounts, management information, existing borrowing details and an explanation of the requirement. The exact list depends on the facility.

What interest rate will I pay?

Pricing depends on the lender, product, risk, term, security and transaction. We focus on the whole facility rather than an isolated headline rate.

Will I need a personal guarantee?

Not always. Requirements vary by lender and facility. A personal guarantee is a significant legal commitment and should be understood before signing.

Why would a profitable business need working capital?

Profit and cash are different. Growth can absorb cash through stock, suppliers, wages and customer payment terms.

What is invoice finance?

Funding against eligible unpaid customer invoices, allowing businesses to access cash before normal payment dates.

How is an RCF different from a loan?

A term loan is usually advanced as a lump sum. An RCF provides an agreed limit that can be drawn, repaid and potentially redrawn.

Can I finance used equipment?

Potentially. Lenders consider the asset type, age, condition, value and expected useful life.

What is bridging finance?

Short-term secured funding often used when speed matters or longer-term finance is not yet appropriate.

Can I borrow to buy another business?

Potentially. Acquisition structures can combine term debt, ABL, invoice finance, asset finance, property-backed borrowing, vendor finance and purchaser equity.

Why use a commercial finance broker?

A broker can help structure the requirement, identify lenders whose appetite fits, present the proposal and manage the process through to completion.

Can you guarantee approval?

No. Finance remains subject to lender criteria, underwriting, due diligence and final approval.
