GETTING STARTED

What to have ready.

Have recent business bank statements, accounts or management figures, details of existing borrowing and the funding purpose ready. The lender may request further information.

We act as an introducer to finance providers. We do not provide financial advice. Finance is subject to status and lender approval; terms and security requirements vary.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

Can a small business apply for a business loan?

Lenders assess trading history, turnover, affordability, existing borrowing and credit history. Tell us the amount, purpose and timing so we can explore relevant providers. Eligibility differs between lenders.

Does an unsecured business loan require a personal guarantee?

Unsecured does not mean there is no personal liability. A lender may still require a director’s personal guarantee. Review the proposed security and guarantee terms before committing.

Can you explore funding if my bank has declined?

A bank decline does not establish every lender’s position. We can discuss the reason for the decline and whether another funding route may be relevant. Approval is never guaranteed.
