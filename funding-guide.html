YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

What documents do I need for a business loan?

A useful starting point is recent business bank statements, accounts or management figures, existing borrowing details and a clear funding purpose. Requirements vary by product and lender. Property, asset and invoice facilities need transaction-specific information too.

How much can my business borrow?

There is no single amount available to every business. Providers consider affordability, turnover, trading history, credit, security and existing commitments. Start with the amount needed and a realistic repayment plan.

Can I explore finance after a bank decline?

Yes, we can discuss the reason for the decline and whether other providers or structures may be relevant. A different provider may reach a different decision, but funding is not guaranteed.

Can a business with adverse credit enquire?

You can discuss the circumstances with us. The type, age and status of credit issues affect lender appetite. Be clear about arrears, defaults or judgments so the requirement can be considered accurately; acceptance is not guaranteed.

Can a new or early-stage business enquire?

Yes, but limited trading history can narrow the available options. Tell us when trading began, revenue to date, the funding purpose and available contribution. We will explain whether there is a relevant route within our service.

How should I compare business finance costs?

Compare total repayable, payment frequency, interest calculation, arrangement and ongoing fees, early repayment terms and security. A lower headline rate alone does not establish a lower overall cost. Refinancing over a longer term may reduce payments while increasing total cost.

Will I need to provide a personal guarantee?

Some providers require personal guarantees, including for unsecured borrowing. Understand the scope of any guarantee and obtain independent legal advice before signing if needed.

How quickly can funding complete?

Timing depends on the product, documents, lender checks and any valuation or legal work. Tell us your deadline at the outset. We cannot promise approval or completion within a fixed timescale.
