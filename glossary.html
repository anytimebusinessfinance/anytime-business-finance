Finance Glossary

Commercial finance. Explained clearly.

Search plain-English explanations of the terminology used across commercial lending, working capital, assets and property.

A

Acquisition Finance

Funding used to help purchase another business, company, division or shareholding.

Advance Rate

The percentage of an eligible asset value against which a lender is prepared to provide funding.

Amortisation

The gradual repayment of borrowing over an agreed period.

Arrangement Fee

A charge associated with establishing or arranging a finance facility.

Asset-Based Lending (ABL)

Funding supported by eligible assets within a business, potentially including receivables, stock, machinery and property.

Asset Finance

Funding used to acquire business assets while spreading the cost over time.

Asset Refinance

Finance raised against assets a business already owns.

Auction Finance

Short-term property finance designed around auction completion timescales.

Availability

The amount that can actually be drawn from a facility at a particular time.

B

Balloon Payment

A larger final payment due at the end of a finance agreement.

Base Rate

The Bank of England policy interest rate; some facilities are priced as Bank Rate plus a margin.

Borrowing Base

The pool or value of eligible assets used to calculate how much can be drawn.

Bridging Finance

Short-term secured finance commonly used where speed or an interim funding solution is required.

Business Loan

Funding provided to a business for an agreed commercial purpose.

C

Cash Conversion Cycle

The time it takes to convert operating expenditure back into cash received from customers.

Cash Flow

The movement of money into and out of a business.

Commercial Mortgage

Longer-term finance secured against commercial property.

Concentration Limit

A restriction on how much facility availability can depend on one debtor or concentrated exposure.

Covenant

A contractual condition within a finance agreement.

Credit Appetite

The types of borrowers and transactions a lender is prepared to consider.

D

Debenture

Company security that can create fixed and/or floating charges over assets.

Debt Service

Payments required to meet borrowing obligations.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

A ratio comparing available cash flow with required debt service.

Debt-to-EBITDA

A leverage ratio comparing relevant debt with EBITDA.

Debtor Days

An indication of how long customers take to pay invoices.

Development Finance

Funding for property development projects.

Drawdown

Accessing some or all of an approved finance facility.

E

EBITDA

Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation.

Adjusted EBITDA

EBITDA modified for accepted exceptional, non-recurring or normalising adjustments.

Equity

Ownership value after relevant liabilities.

Exit Strategy

The planned method by which short-term borrowing is expected to be repaid.

F

Factoring

Invoice finance where the funder typically also undertakes credit control and collections.

Facility Limit

The maximum approved size of a facility, subject to its terms and availability.

Finance Lease

An asset-finance structure under which the business leases an asset for an agreed period.

Fixed Charge

Security attached to a specific asset or identifiable class of assets.

Floating Charge

Security over a changing pool of company assets.

G

Gearing

A measure of financial leverage.

Gross Development Value (GDV)

The estimated market value of a completed property development.

Gross Profit

Revenue less direct cost of generating that revenue.

H

Hire Purchase

Asset finance involving scheduled payments with ownership typically transferring after contractual requirements are met.

HMO Finance

Specialist property finance for Houses in Multiple Occupation.

Hunting Pot Facility

Pre-agreed funding capacity for eligible property investors or developers to pursue future qualifying acquisitions.

I

Interest Cover

A measure of how comfortably earnings or cash flow can cover interest expense.

Interest-Only

A structure where regular payments cover interest while capital remains outstanding.

Invoice Discounting

Funding against eligible receivables while the business generally retains collections.

Invoice Finance

Funding provided against eligible outstanding customer invoices.

L

Leverage

The extent to which debt is used to finance a business or transaction.

Loan-to-Cost (LTC)

Borrowing compared with the total cost of a project.

Loan-to-GDV (LTGDV)

Development borrowing compared with expected gross development value.

Loan-to-Value (LTV)

Borrowing compared with the value of the asset securing it.

M

Management Accounts

Internal financial statements showing recent business performance.

Management Buyout (MBO)

An acquisition by members of the existing management team.

Margin

The percentage added by a lender to a reference interest rate.

Mezzanine Finance

Funding that sits between senior debt and equity in a capital structure.

P

Personal Guarantee (PG)

A personal commitment to meet specified business obligations if the borrowing company does not.

Purchase Order Finance

Funding designed to help fulfil qualifying confirmed customer orders.

R

Receivables

Amounts owed to a business by customers.

Refinancing

Replacing or restructuring existing debt.

Revolving Credit Facility (RCF)

A facility that can be drawn, repaid and potentially redrawn during its term.

S

Sale & Leaseback

Selling an asset and leasing it back to release capital while retaining use.

Secured Lending

Finance supported by security over specified assets.

SONIA

Sterling Overnight Index Average, a benchmark used in sterling finance.

Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV)

A legal entity established for a specific purpose or transaction.

Stock Finance

Funding supported by or used to acquire inventory.

T

Term Loan

A loan advanced for a defined period and repaid under an agreed schedule.

Term Sheet

A summary of proposed finance terms before definitive legal documentation.

Trade Finance

Funding and banking solutions supporting the purchase and sale of goods.

Turnover

Total revenue generated over a specified period before expenses.

U

Unsecured Business Loan

Business borrowing not secured by a specific identified asset.

Utilisation

The amount of an approved facility currently drawn.

V

VAT Finance

Short-term funding used to meet a VAT liability while spreading its cash-flow impact.

Vendor Finance

Funding or deferred consideration provided directly or indirectly by a seller.

W

Working Capital

Capital required to support day-to-day trading and the timing gap between costs and receipts.

Working Capital Cycle

The movement of cash through suppliers, stock/work, invoicing and customer receipts.

Y

Yield

Income generated by an investment relative to value or cost, usually expressed as a percentage.
