FINANCE GLOSSARY

Commercial finance, explained.

Clear definitions of common funding and lending terms.

ABL

Asset-based lending: finance secured against business assets such as receivables, stock, plant or property.

Bridging Finance

Short-term secured finance commonly used for property purchases, auctions, refurbishment or time-sensitive transactions.

DSCR

Debt Service Coverage Ratio compares cash available for debt service with required debt repayments.

EBITDA

Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation; commonly used as an indicator of operating performance.

Invoice Finance

Funding that releases cash against unpaid customer invoices.

Personal Guarantee

A commitment from an individual to meet specified business obligations if the business cannot.

Revolving Credit Facility

A pre-agreed credit limit that may be drawn, repaid and potentially redrawn during the facility term.

Security

Assets or rights a lender may take as support for a finance facility.

Working Capital

Funds available to support the day-to-day operating cycle of a business.
