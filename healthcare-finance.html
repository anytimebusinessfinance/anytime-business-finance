GETTING STARTED

What to have ready.

Bring a business summary, recent financial information and details of the acquisition, premises or equipment. For an acquisition, include the target’s accounts and the proposed purchase structure.

We act as an introducer to finance providers. We do not provide financial advice. Finance is subject to status and lender approval; terms and security requirements vary.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

Can you explore finance to buy a healthcare practice?

We can discuss practice acquisitions and partner buy-ins or buyouts. Lenders consider the target business, financial performance, purchase structure, experience and available contribution.

Can medical or dental equipment be financed?

Equipment finance may be relevant for qualifying clinical, diagnostic or dental assets. Supplier details, equipment value, deposit and the business’s financial position affect available options.

What about premises, fit-outs and cash flow?

Healthcare funding can involve separate property, equipment and working capital requirements. We help present those requirements to relevant providers, subject to scope and lender criteria.
