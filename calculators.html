How these estimates are calculated

Loan payments fall at the end of each month or quarter. For finance leases, the initial rental is separate from the number of further monthly rentals; an entered final rental is additional to those rentals. Lease totals exclude VAT and secondary rentals. Quarterly terms must be multiples of 3 months; all terms are limited to 600 months. Revolving credit daily interest is simple, without daily compounding. Average monthly interest uses 365 days per year divided into 12 equal periods, rather than actual calendar dates. Bridging serviced and retained interest is simple; rolled-up interest compounds monthly. Rates are assumed constant throughout the term. Calculations use full precision; displayed figures are rounded to pennies and may not sum exactly. Flat annual interest is charged on the original interest basis for the term. Factor charges fix the total charge and do not compound. Net interest basis excludes upfront fees and financed fees, but does not deduct retained interest from the interest basis.

Important: These calculators provide illustrative figures only. They are not a finance offer, quotation or recommendation.
