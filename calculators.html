Calculation assumptions: Payments fall at the end of each month or quarter. Quarterly terms must be multiples of 3 months; all terms are limited to 600 months. Daily rates use 365 days per year converted to equal monthly or quarterly periods, rather than actual calendar-day accrual. Calculations use full precision; displayed figures are rounded to pennies and may not sum exactly. Flat and factor charges are allocated across the term and do not compound. Net interest basis excludes upfront fees and financed fees, but does not deduct retained interest from the interest basis. Important: These calculators provide illustrative figures only. They are not a finance offer, quotation or recommendation. Actual repayments, interest calculations, fees, security requirements and facility terms vary by lender and product.
