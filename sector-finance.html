BUSINESS SECTORS

Start with your trading cycle.

Construction & trades

Plant and machinery purchases may point to asset finance. Upfront labour, materials and staged contract payments need a separate working capital discussion.

Recruitment & staffing

Weekly payroll and longer customer payment terms can create a funding gap. Invoice finance may be relevant where invoices meet provider criteria.

Transport & logistics

Vehicle and HGV funding can support fleet investment. Funding customer invoices may help with timing gaps around fuel, wages and operating costs.

Wholesale & manufacturing
