Website analytics

When enabled with your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand website visits and interactions, including finance pages viewed, calculator types used, illustration actions and enquiry actions. Google processes technical information such as browser and device details and uses online identifiers for visit statistics. Advertising features are disabled.

Our custom analytics events do not contain your name, contact details, enquiry text or exact calculator inputs. A form submit attempt is recorded separately from a successfully received enquiry; confirmed enquiries will only be measured once delivery is connected.

You can refuse optional analytics or withdraw consent through “Analytics settings” in the footer. See our cookie information for browser storage and retention settings. Google may process analytics information outside the UK under its applicable data protection arrangements. The final analytics account settings and wider privacy notice must be reviewed before launch.
