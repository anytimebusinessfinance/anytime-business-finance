YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

What finance could help with a cash flow gap?

The cause of the gap matters. Unpaid invoices, seasonal stock purchases and upfront contract costs can point to different facilities. We start with the cash cycle and the expected source of repayment.

Can funding help with supplier or stock purchases?

Trade, inventory or purchase order finance may be relevant to some transactions. Providers consider the goods, supplier, customer, margins and how the transaction will repay the facility.

Is invoice finance the same as a working capital loan?

No. Invoice finance is linked to eligible invoices, while a loan has its own agreed repayment structure. Our invoice finance guide explains the main facility types and costs to compare.
