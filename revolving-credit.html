REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITIES

A facility for changing cash needs.

A revolving credit facility, also called a business line of credit, provides an agreed funding limit. We consider the credit limit, expected use and available security. Drawdown rules, renewal terms and fees matter as much as the headline rate.

Unsecured Revolving Credit

A flexible credit limit that may be drawn, repaid and reused without specific business assets being taken as security, subject to lender terms.

Secured Revolving Credit

Revolving working capital supported by suitable business or property security, potentially allowing a larger or differently structured facility.

Receivables-Backed Facilities

Flexible funding supported by eligible trade receivables, linking available capital to the value of the debtor book.

Asset-Backed Facilities

Facilities supported by qualifying business assets, which can include combinations of receivables, stock, plant, machinery or property.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

How does a business line of credit work?

An approved facility provides access to funding within agreed limits. The ability to draw, repay and redraw depends on the agreement, continuing eligibility and any review conditions.

What costs should I compare?

Look at interest on drawings alongside arrangement, commitment, non-utilisation, renewal and other applicable fees. Compare the total expected cost for how you intend to use the facility.

Can a facility be secured against business assets?

Some facilities use eligible receivables, stock, equipment or property as security. Valuation, existing charges and lender criteria affect the structure and available limit.
