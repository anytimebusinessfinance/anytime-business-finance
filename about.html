About Anytime Business Finance

Business finance starts with understanding the business.

The right funding starts with a conversation: understand the business, its challenges and ambitions, then identify the structure and lenders that fit.

Built around experienced, personal advice.

Anytime Business Finance was founded to combine access to commercial finance with experienced, personal advice built around each business.

Experience on both sides of the table.

Founder Sam Percival's experience spans both lending and commercial finance broking. Having worked directly for lenders, he understands how funders assess businesses, analyse financial performance and cash flow, evaluate security and structure facilities.

He has also worked directly with business owners, sitting down to understand how their businesses operate, the challenges they face and what they are trying to achieve.

His experience spans a broad range of commercial finance, with particular expertise in working capital and asset-based lending, including revolving credit facilities, invoice finance, asset finance, secured lending and structured funding solutions.

Clients deal directly with someone who understands both what a business needs from its funding and what a lender needs to see to provide it.
