RELEASE CASH FROM INVOICES

Waiting for customers to pay?

Growing sales can create a cash flow gap when wages and suppliers fall due before customers settle invoices. Invoice finance may release part of the value of eligible invoices earlier. The balance is settled according to the agreement, after applicable charges.

We discuss your customers, payment terms, ledger and existing facilities before introducing the requirement to relevant providers.

COMPARE THE WHOLE FACILITY

Look beyond the headline rate.

Compare the service fee, discount charge, arrangement fees and any annual minimum. Check the advance rate, customer concentration limits, funding exclusions, contract term and notice period.

If bad debt protection is offered, understand its limits, exclusions and claim conditions. It should not be assumed to cover every unpaid invoice.

What to have ready

Recent accounts or management figures, an aged debtor report, customer payment terms, turnover, invoice examples and details of existing funding will help start the discussion.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

Could invoice finance suit recruitment, transport or wholesale?

It may be relevant where a business issues eligible business-to-business invoices on credit terms. The provider assesses the sector, customers, invoice quality, disputes and trading performance.

Will my customers know about the facility?

This depends on whether the arrangement is disclosed or confidential and how collections are handled. Discuss your requirements before choosing a facility.

Do I have to change an existing invoice finance provider?

No. We can discuss a new facility or a possible review of existing arrangements. Check current minimum fees, notice periods, termination costs and security before considering a change.
