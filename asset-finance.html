YOUR QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you apply.

Can I finance used equipment or machinery?

Some providers consider used assets. Age, condition, supplier, value and expected working life affect lender appetite and the available term.

How do hire purchase and leasing differ?

Ownership, end-of-term options and payment structures differ between agreements. Compare the deposit, total payable, final payments and what happens to the asset when the agreement ends.

Can existing assets help release working capital?

Asset refinance may release capital against qualifying assets already owned by the business. Valuation, existing finance and lender security requirements affect what may be available.
